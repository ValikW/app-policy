Privacy Policy

Applies to the app "Валера строит" (a Threads API business app). It is a single-user tool run by its owner, a solo creator in the AI/automation niche (Threads: @valikvw). It has no consumer users.

What data the app uses

  • Data of the owner's own Threads account (profile, own posts) via the Threads API.
  • Public Threads posts and public profile information of other creators in the same niche, retrieved through keyword search and profile lookup.

Why

To analyze which public posts perform well in the niche (competitor analysis), to show the results privately to the owner, and to publish the owner's own posts.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored locally on the owner's computer. It is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising or to train AI models. Results are shown only to the owner in a private Telegram chat.

Deletion

Stored data is deleted on request, see data deletion instructions.
